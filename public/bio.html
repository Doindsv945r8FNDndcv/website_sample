About

I started in print, moved to software, and stayed for the research.

I'm a product designer working where interface design meets user research. Most of my work is on tools people use all day: dashboards, internal systems, clinical software. In that kind of work, being clear matters more than being clever.

Right now I lead design at Fieldnote, a small team building research software. Outside work I teach an evening interaction design course and take a lot of photographs at night.
